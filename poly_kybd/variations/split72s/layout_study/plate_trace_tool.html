Plate trace tool

Drag the plate to move it. Shift + wheel: rotate. Alt + wheel: scale. Wheel: zoom view. Drag elsewhere: pan. Arrow keys nudge by 1 px.

Load a photo (button above, or drop a file on the photo area).

Placement

Scale is plate mm per photo pixel. Stretch above 1 shows less of the photo along that axis, so the glyphs get wider (x) or taller (y).

Coverage: - of the plate lies on the tablet

The mask counts photo pixels darker than the mask value (after blurring) as tablet. Optimise searches angle +-3 deg and centre +-120 px around the current placement, keeping scale and stretch.

Tracing

Background: radius of the blur that is subtracted before the threshold; it removes uneven lighting. Threshold: grey levels above the background. Strokes thinner than the silk min width are shown red.
