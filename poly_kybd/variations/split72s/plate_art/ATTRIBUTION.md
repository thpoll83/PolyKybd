# Attribution: split72s plate artwork

The plate silkscreen artwork in this folder is traced from a photograph of the verso of
Rongorongo Tablet B (Aruku-Kurenga), the wooden tablet from Rapa Nui (Easter Island).

| | |
|---|---|
| Object | Rongorongo Tablet B, Aruku-Kurenga, verso. Held by the Congregation of the Sacred Hearts of Jesus and Mary |
| Photograph | Trocadéro Ethnographic Museum, Paris |
| First published | Stéphen-Charles Chauvet, *L'île de Pâques et ses mystères*, Éditions Tel, Paris, 1935 |
| Source of the file | Wikimedia Commons, [File:Rongorongo_B-v_Aruku-Kurenga.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rongorongo_B-v_Aruku-Kurenga.jpg), from the 2004 online translation of Chauvet's book by Ann Altman. The copy used here is the same scan from the Internet Archive item [rongorongotexts](https://archive.org/details/rongorongotexts) |
